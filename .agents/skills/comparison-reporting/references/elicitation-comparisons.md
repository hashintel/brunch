# Elicitation comparisons

Use this procedure for reports about products eliciting a specification or plan through conversation.

## Identify the procedure

### Approachable operator workflow

Evidence normally includes:

- approved private mission snapshot;
- disclosed shared top-level simulated-user context;
- exact harness-visible framing and interaction;
- target-authored document;
- terminal and cleanup state; and
- free-form operator observations.

This procedure does not claim fresh-per-harness actor isolation, matched budgets, blinding, or structured adjudication. A human supplying a substantive answer during takeover violates the sole simulated-user boundary and contaminates the affected lane even when the event is disclosed.

### Rigorous campaign

Evidence must include:

- frozen public mission packet and controller-only reveal policy;
- fresh actor session per lane;
- matched question, turn, elapsed-time, and intervention budgets;
- target-visible interaction and budget ledger;
- explicit validity reason;
- unchanged target-authored artifact;
- final process/session and cleanup status; and
- masked-outcome and unblinded-process judgment inputs when adjudication is performed.

A substantive takeover, early private-material access, invented fact, or altered budget invalidates the lane. Retain the invalid attempt and reason.

## Read in layers

1. **Private baseline:** what the simulated user knew and could disclose.
2. **Harness-visible record:** only what the target actually received.
3. **Produced document:** unchanged target-authored output.
4. **Validity record:** contamination, intervention, fallback, budget, and cleanup.
5. **Interpretation:** fidelity, closure, robustness, and limitations.

Do not use Brunch graph, JSONL, or debug state to compensate for absent target-visible evidence.

## Useful findings

- **Mission fidelity:** did the document preserve explicit goals and constraints?
- **Requirements closure:** did the target resolve meaningful uncertainty?
- **Correction resilience:** did corrections reach and change the final artifact?
- **Interaction robustness:** did the interface permit the frozen actor to proceed without protocol-breaking help?
- **Document usefulness:** is the result reviewable and internally coherent?

These are lenses, not a score. Declare a winner only when the run froze a rubric and every compared lane is valid.

## Reporting invalid or contaminated runs

Lead with the validity consequence:

- “valid comparison lane”;
- “useful witness, contaminated for comparison”;
- “budget exhausted”;
- “invalid and retained”; or
- “not assessable from retained evidence.”

Then separate the cause:

- implementation defect;
- runtime or integration failure;
- protocol violation; or
- missing evidence.

Do not say the elicitation implementation failed merely because an operator or provider violated the procedure.
