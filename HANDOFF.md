#### B. Answered cards now show the right *categories* of information, but the capture line is too coarse

Observed in the same framing screenshot.

Conclusion:
- the `Chosen` and `Context` sections are directionally correct
- `Captured: Workspace knowledge updated from this answer.` is not good enough
- the server/database already links captured knowledge items to turns (`turn_knowledge_item` with relation `captured`), so the missing seam is **projection**, not persistence
- the next capture-oriented slice should expose the actual linked items per turn

Important unresolved design constraint:
- simple single-letter identifiers like `C7` will not work because `context`, `constraint`, and `criterion` all collide on `C`
- a compact but non-colliding identifier scheme is needed (still volatile; not yet committed to `memory/SPEC.md`)

#### C. The final cards in the framing screenshot are wrong because closure/control turns are being rendered as answered question cards

Observed near the end of:
- `/Users/lunelson/Library/Application Support/CleanShot/media/media_JbebqjgwOJ/CleanShot 2026-04-14 at 16.04.05@2x.png`

Conclusion:
- this is likely not just a fixture problem
- the renderer is currently too permissive: any answered turn can collapse into an answered-turn card
- but phase-close summaries / confirmations are a different interaction family and should render through dedicated phase-terminal artifacts instead

#### D. Open phases should never bottom out in an empty/start CTA

This is the most important product correction from the latest review.

Conclusion:
- if a phase is open and unresolved, the bottom-most thing in the pane should be:
  - the current unanswered question, or
  - a visible generation state while the next question is being prepared
- if a phase is closed, the bottom-most thing should be a handoff/completion card
- therefore `Begin/Continue` is no longer the right primary affordance for an already-open phase

Conclusions that are now durable enough to drive planning:
- replay should be **turn-shaped**, not message-shaped
- generic user answer bubbles are an anti-pattern for structured elicitation replay
- `step-start` markers have no meaningful replay value and should stay hidden
- completed turns should collapse into compact answered-turn cards
- observer status belongs to the turn that produced it, not to a free-floating transcript row
- interviewer completion can likely unlock the next turn before observer capture completes, as long as the observer's trailing state stays attached to the collapsed answered-turn card
