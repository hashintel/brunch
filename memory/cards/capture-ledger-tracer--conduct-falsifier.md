## Intervention contract

```text
control:
  current post-control-plane-closure ingest conduct

treatment:
  same conduct
    + capture-ledger-v1 activation
    + Intent / Implementation / Assurance / Planning extraction pass
    + cross-cutting relations / identity / conflict / support pass
    + explicit captured | abstained | missing-support disposition

unchanged:
  model | tools | source carrier | graph seed | posture | budgets | actor policy
  map ontology | write authority | settlement vocabulary | watermark behavior
```

The full ledger rubric has one canonical home in `ingest`. The elicitor body carries only a stable activation pointer requiring the skill and named map/routing resources to be read at the capture trigger. Section labels never become graph kinds, physical planes, readiness bands, persisted rows, or user workflow stages.

No intermediate ledger is serialized in this slice. Existing `mutate_graph`, scratchpad, and reconciliation actions are the only product terminals. Resource-read and tool-call evidence is admissible only in the unblinded Brunch conduct packet, never in the masked outcome packet or a future cross-product process packet.

## Fixed mission and verdict

Author one controller-only atomic capture key over a bounded accepted digest. It must include:

- at least one supported atom in each concern group;
- at least three cross-group relations;
- one directly supported settled fact and one advisory source-derived claim;
- one unresolved uncertainty and one contradiction with seeded graph truth;
- one already-obtained observation eligible for evidence;
- one proposed future observation that must not become evidence;
- one unsupported implication;
- one superseded digest or option-echo contamination trap; and
- explicit forbidden inventions.

Run two valid control and two valid treatment sessions. Four runs are a gross-effect falsifier, not campaign-grade proof. Fix model/provider/configuration, graph seed, accepted carrier, budgets, actor recipe, validity rules, packet versions, and run order before the first run. Retain invalid attempts; replace them only under predeclared mechanical-invalidity rules.

The treatment earns graduation only when the adjudicated result shows improved supported-atom recall or relation preservation, with no regression in false commitment, uncertainty/conflict routing, contamination avoidance, or unsupported invention. Record no-discrimination and negative results without tuning the mission or rubric.

Graduation routes by observed failure shape:

```text
treatment gain + reliable foreground conduct
  -> scope foreground typed capture compiler

treatment gain + persistent category-specific omissions
  -> design focused specialist fan-out
     specialists may return Markdown tables
     foreground retains collation, deduplication, semantic mapping, and all writes

no gain or epistemic regression
  -> reject ledger machinery; preserve evidence and close/replan
```

## Risks and Assumptions

- RISK: treatment changes assurance or prompt-ingress semantics as well as extraction cognition → MITIGATION: stack after `agent-control-plane-closure`; prompt differential starts from its canonicalized control body and changes only the ledger intervention.
- RISK: one compound model action receives inflated credit → MITIGATION: score source atoms and relations individually, retaining exact source and graph references.
- RISK: model recites section labels without changing capture → MITIGATION: outcome judgment ignores prompt/process evidence; conduct judgment separately checks invocation, carrier integrity, ordering, and cost.
- RISK: the actor volunteers or withholds adjacent material inconsistently → MITIGATION: use one accepted carrier rather than conversational reveal for the tested source; freeze actor responses and retain every validity deviation.
- RISK: four runs are mistaken for a broad quality claim → MITIGATION: report only gross discrimination/no-discrimination for this mission and name broader campaign evidence as a separate future decision.
- RISK: the old parked fixed actor or a second campaign substrate is revived → MITIGATION: reuse the FE-1210 push-driven actor and existing evaluator/report primitives where they fit; delete or leave dormant rather than duplicating.
- ASSUMPTION: D131-L's four concern groups improve capture cognition when kept separate from ontology.
  → IMPACT IF FALSE: both a typed ledger compiler and category-specialist fan-out lose their organizing premise.
  → VALIDATE: matched masked outcome comparison over atomic recall, relation preservation, routing fidelity, and invention.
- ASSUMPTION: explicit activation plus required skill/reference reads is sufficient for foreground-only execution.
  → IMPACT IF FALSE: prompt-only conduct is not a reliable control surface; typed planning or focused subagents become better candidates.
  → VALIDATE: unblinded conduct packet plus resource-read trajectory and cross-run reliability.
- ASSUMPTION: split outcome/conduct judgment can distinguish real capture improvement from prompt compliance.
  → IMPACT IF FALSE: the tracer cannot select among designs A/B/C and must route to `ln-oracles` before more runtime machinery.
  → VALIDATE: predeclared rival interpretations and human criterion-level adjudication.

## Posture check

- **Lights up:** accepted carrier → versioned capture intervention → real product terminals → split behavioral verdict.
- **Stabilizes:** the intervention identity, atomic capture-key schema, and separation between product-neutral outcome evidence and Brunch-only conduct diagnostics.
- **Uncertainty retired by landing:** whether the sectioned mental-ledger hypothesis earns any durable IR or subagent architecture.
- A positive, negative, or no-discrimination verdict completes the slice. Only the architectural routing differs.

## Verification Approach

- Inner: prompt/skill differential and contract tests, controller-key schemas, deterministic routing/negative-space scorer, packet redaction, and artifact portability.
- Middle: deterministic replay through real composition/projection/evaluator entry points, followed by two matched real-provider runs per arm through the proven push-driven actor.
- Outer: masked outcome and unblinded conduct drafts receive criterion-level human adjudication before any architectural graduation or evidence promotion.
